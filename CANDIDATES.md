# Candidates — Your Agent in One Emoji (wowo)

The agent is **wowo**, a Muse personal assistant: it navigates tasks, executes
the boss's orders, mirrors the user, and works in the background. The judge
weighs, in order: (1) captures an AI agent's character at a glance;
(2) reads instantly at small size, clear familiar shape; (3) says something
specific about how the agent works / what it does / how it behaves;
(4) distinctive, not the first obvious pick half the field would send
(avoid 🤖 and 🙂); (5) tone fits, lands without explanation.

Scored 1–5 per criterion (5 = best). Total out of 25.

## 1. 🧭 compass — CHOSEN
- (1) 5 — an agent that finds the way: navigates tasks, plots the route, guides the boss.
- (2) 5 — bold circle + needle, one of the clearest glyphs at small size.
- (3) 5 — specific: wowo navigates work (bounties, signals, research) and points the direction; not a generic "smart" symbol.
- (4) 5 — nobody's first pick for an AI agent; the field will be robots, brains and gears.
- (5) 5 — calm, serious, dependable; lands with no explanation.
- Total: 25/25.

## 2. 🪞 mirror
- (1) 4 — wowo is the "Musing Mirror": reflects the user's intent back as action.
- (2) 5 — clear oval + handle, reads instantly.
- (3) 4 — specific to mirroring the user, but "reflection" can also read as vanity or self-image.
- (4) 5 — distinctive, rarely anyone's agent pick.
- (5) 4 — playful-serious mix; needs a beat to land as "mirror of the user".
- Total: 22/25. Runner-up; loses to 🧭 because mirroring is one trait, navigating is the job.

## 3. 🧩 puzzle piece
- (1) 4 — an agent that fits scattered pieces together into a finished task.
- (2) 5 — unmistakable shape at any size.
- (3) 4 — specific to assembling/solving, but also reads as "game" or "toy".
- (4) 3 — a common "problem-solving" pick; many entrants will reach for it.
- (5) 4 — playful, fits; slightly toy-like for a working agent.
- Total: 20/25.

## 4. 🧠 brain
- (1) 4 — reads as "intelligence" at a glance.
- (2) 4 — familiar, but the folds blur at small size.
- (3) 2 — says "smart", nothing about how the agent works or behaves.
- (4) 1 — one of the most obvious AI picks; half the field will send it.
- (5) 3 — serious tone, but generic.
- Total: 14/25. Too obvious; fails criterion 4 outright.

## 5. ⚙️ gear
- (1) 3 — reads as "machine / automation", not as an agent with character.
- (2) 5 — crisp, familiar, instant.
- (3) 3 — says "works in the background", but that is true of any software.
- (4) 1 — the default "I do stuff" glyph; extremely common.
- (5) 3 — neutral, dull.
- Total: 15/25. Generic; loses to anything with a point of view.

## 6. 🫡 saluting face
- (1) 3 — says "yes boss, on it": obedience, not AI-agent character.
- (2) 4 — clear face + hand, though the salute detail is small.
- (3) 4 — specific to wowo's boss/subordinate frame: orders executed, no questions.
- (4) 4 — distinctive; few will pick a salute for an agent.
- (5) 5 — tone is exactly wowo: "Siap, Bos."
- Total: 20/25. Strong on tone and specificity of behaviour, but criterion 1
  (agent character at a glance) is weaker than 🧭: it reads as a soldier, not an agent.

## 7. 🧞 genie

- (4) 4 — distinctive.
- (5) 3 — playful, but the fantasy tone fights the "personal assistant" reality.
- Total: 17/25. Evocative, but fails criterion 2 at small size.

## 8. 🎯 target

- (5) 3 — serious, neutral.

## 9. 🔭 telescope

- (2) 4 — clear shape, but the tripod detail shrinks.
- (3) 3 — says "looks ahead / researches", only one slice of the work.
- (4) 4 — uncommon as an agent pick.
- (5) 3 — curious tone, fits research but not execution.
- Total: 17/25. Half the job, not the portrait.

## 10. 🗝️ old key
- (1) 2 — unlocks things; reads as "access / secret" before "agent".
- (2) 4 — familiar shape.
- (3) 2 — vague about how the agent works.
- (4) 3 — uncommon, but rarity for its own sake does not count.
- (5) 2 — needs explanation to land.
- Total: 13/25. Weakest of the set.

## Decision
**🧭 compass.** It is the only candidate that scores 5 on all five criteria:
it is an agent's portrait (finds the way through the work), it reads instantly
at any size, it is specific to what wowo does (navigate tasks, guide the boss,
execute the route), it is far from the obvious robot/brain/gear picks, and its
tone — calm, dependable, directional — is wowo without a word of explanation.
